### Motivation
Crafting product presentations is often a time sink, involving brainstorming, copywriting, and video production. If I could jot down your basic ideas, and AI guides the rest of the way to success...

### Product

Quinvio is a web app that streamlines the creation of video presentations for businesses with the power of LLMs and synthetic media.

Tailored for product owners and Go-to-Market specialists, Quinvio offers a cost-effective solution to automate brainstorming, copywriting, and video production using realistic avatars, voices, and visuals.
### Journey

- __Dec 2022__: The initial spark
- __Feb 2023__: MVP launch on [ProductHunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/quinvio)
- __June 2024__: Reached 20K users
- __May 2024__: Shut down
